Request deletion of your data.

You can request deletion of data held by the MyalgoDesk agent platform, including data obtained through our NRMYALGO Meta integration.

  1. Email manjeetgill01@gmail.com with the subject MyalgoDesk data deletion.
  2. Identify the business or app you used and the WhatsApp number or account associated with your request. Include approximate conversation dates if relevant, and say whether you want a specific conversation removed or your entire platform connection and associated data reviewed for deletion.
  3. We will verify your ownership or authority using the minimum information needed, clarify the scope when necessary and confirm the result by email. Requests are processed manually; submitting an email does not immediately delete records.

Do not include passwords, access tokens, OTPs or identity-document scans in your initial email. If additional verification is necessary, we will explain the appropriate next step.

What the request covers

Depending on the request, we review connection credentials, phone and account identifiers, conversation messages and replies, uploaded business knowledge, stored answers, task records and related audit records held by this platform. A business-wide request requires authority over that business. We will explain any records that cannot be removed because of an applicable legal requirement.

Disconnecting is different from deleting

Revoking Meta authorization or disconnecting WhatsApp stops the connection but does not by itself erase historical records. Please request deletion explicitly if you also want those records removed.

Copies outside this platform

We cannot remove copies held independently by the business you contacted, Meta, ChakraHQ or another service provider. Contact those parties for their deletion procedures. Backup copies may remain until the relevant backups are replaced.
