Agent platform

AI assistance for business conversations.

MyalgoDesk provides software that connects a business’s app, information and approved tools with AI agents. Businesses can use the platform to answer common questions and assist with workflows such as appointment booking.

WhatsApp connections

A business owner authorizes their WhatsApp Business account through Meta. The connected business provides its FAQs, agent instructions and any booking tools. The platform processes incoming messages and generates replies for that business.

The direct Meta connection is currently a pilot. Public customer onboarding remains subject to Meta verification, permission review and successful end-to-end testing. A connected domain does not by itself activate messaging.
