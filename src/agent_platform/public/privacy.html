Privacy policy

This policy describes how MyalgoDesk’s agent platform handles information when a business connects an app or WhatsApp account and when people interact with its agents. MyalgoDesk is operated by Manjeet Gill, a sole proprietor in Greater Noida West, Uttar Pradesh, India. Our Meta integration is named NRMYALGO.

Information we process

  • Business and connection details: business and user identifiers, contact details, WhatsApp Business account and phone-number identifiers, authorization tokens and connection status.
  • Conversations: sender phone identifiers, incoming messages, agent replies, message identifiers, delivery status and conversation history.
  • Business knowledge and actions: FAQs or documents supplied by the business, retrieved excerpts, requests to connected tools, their results and approvals. A booking app may receive appointment details and contact information needed to complete a requested booking.
  • Operation and security records: task events, connection changes, webhook events, errors, usage and audit records. Servers may log connection information such as IP addresses. Voice features, when used, also process audio and transcripts as needed for the conversation.

How we use information

We use information to connect authorized accounts, receive and answer messages, retrieve the relevant business information, carry out authorized workflows, show results to the connected business, investigate failures and protect the service. Businesses are responsible for configuring their agents and using information appropriately.

Who receives information

The connected business and its authorized app receive information relevant to their conversations and workflows. WhatsApp communications pass through Meta. Connections configured through ChakraHQ also use ChakraHQ. Our hosted platform uses DigitalOcean infrastructure. The configured AI, speech or embedding provider receives the inputs needed to generate answers or process speech; these can include message text, relevant history and business document excerpts. Provider configuration can vary by app and may include Google Gemini, NVIDIA-hosted services or another configured model provider.

These providers and a business’s own systems have their own terms and privacy practices. Processing may take place outside your country. Contact us to identify the providers used for a particular connection. We may also disclose information when required by an applicable legal obligation.

Storage and retention

Connection credentials are stored on the backend and access to app records is scoped to the relevant account. Conversation, task and audit records are retained to operate and troubleshoot the service. The current pilot does not apply a universal automatic expiry to all such records. Deletion requests are handled manually; disconnecting an account does not automatically erase previous messages, documents, answers or audit records.

Deletion from our systems does not remove copies held independently by the connected business, Meta or another provider. Backup copies may remain until those backups are replaced. If a legal requirement prevents deletion of particular records, we will explain the applicable limitation when responding to the request.

Your choices and requests

You can ask to access, correct or delete information associated with your connection or conversation. Business owners can disconnect their account or revoke authorization through the applicable provider. Follow the data-deletion instructions or email manjeetgill01@gmail.com. We may need to verify that you are entitled to make the request.

A business may also hold its own copy of your records; contact it separately for those records. Please do not send passwords, OTPs, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information to an agent or by email.

These information pages

These public information pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts or signup forms. The hosted authorization flow and connected business apps are separate features and process the information described above.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the service’s data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. Contact Manjeet Gill at manjeetgill01@gmail.com for questions about this policy.
